# Demo notes: sources and licences

The 12 notes in `demo/notes/` are taken from public Wikipedia articles (6 Korean, 6 English). Wikipedia text is
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . The authors are the Wikipedia contributors named in the page
history of each source page (link in the table).

Changes made to the original text: only the lead section of each article (the text before its first section heading)
was kept, as plain text without templates, images and reference marks; a title heading and a short header (title,
language, source link, license, license link, change note) were added. The sentences were not reworded. Because of
these changes the notes are adapted material, and they are shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0. The
licenses of the rest of this repository are in `LICENSE` and `RESULTS-LICENSE.md`. The notes contain no data from the
field test's own notes, and no private person.
